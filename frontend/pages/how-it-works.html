How LimitFLOOD Protects Communities

LimitFLOOD combines live meteorological telemetry, soil saturation index computing, and automated emergency dispatches into a hyper-local flood risk warning system.

📍 Location → 📡 Open-Meteo API → 📊 Hydrologic Risk Engine → 🗺️ Interactive Map → 🚨 SOS Dispatch
1

Select & Monitor Location

Users specify any city, locality, or exact GPS coordinates. LimitFLOOD leverages OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding to resolve hyper-local monitoring zones across India.

2

Live Telemetry Collection

Real-time weather API integration pulls 1-hour precipitation intensity, 24-hour accumulated rainfall, surface temperature, wind speed, and topsoil moisture levels (0–1cm depth).

3

Multi-Factor Risk Scoring

Our backend risk engine evaluates rainfall volume (45%), instantaneous rain rate (30%), and soil absorption capacity (25%) to output a transparent 0–100 Flood Risk Index categorized into Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.

4

Interactive Map Visualization

Leaflet.js mapping renders risk contours, radius perimeters, nearby emergency evacuation shelters, and live occupancy status in real time.

5

Alerts & Warning Notices

Active monitoring automatically triggers flash flood alerts and safe shelter route navigation as rainfall thresholds exceed critical boundaries.

6

Emergency Contact & SOS Network

Pressing and holding the Emergency SOS button dispatches automated distress broadcasts with live GPS location to configured family and safety circle contacts.
